03 / 16

Logo

With logo Without logo

The mark is a circle and a square of equal height. Keep half the mark height clear around the logo, and two thirds after the name, so the open end of the word balances the mark.

With no logo, the name set in the house face is the logo. These rules fix its weight, tracking and the full stop.

LockupsMin. 24 px mark
  1. APrimary. Ink on paper. Use it wherever you can.
  2. BStacked. For square and narrow formats, avatars and stickers.
  3. COn ink. Paper on the ink colour, for dark screens and the back of cards.
  4. DReversed. White on the accent, once per spread at most.
  5. EMark only. When the name is already on screen, and below 120 px of lockup width.

Weight 600, tracking −6.5%

Sentence case only

Voice

Writing

Write the way the page looks: short, plain, nothing that doesn’t carry weight. Say what happened and what to do next.

Plain words
“Use”, not “leverage”. “Send”, not “submit”. If a word needs a tooltip, pick another one.
Sentence case
Headings, buttons and labels all start with one capital. Mono labels are the only capitals.
Verbs on buttons
A button says what it does: “Get the files”, never “OK” or “Click here”.
